# PainTalk

### Visual pain communication for people who may have difficulty communicating verbally.

PainTalk is an accessibility-focused mobile application built with Flutter and Dart to help people communicate approximately where and how much they are experiencing pain when verbal communication is difficult.

The project was created from a real-world accessibility problem and developed as a human-centered, offline-first communication aid.

> PainTalk is a communication aid — not a diagnostic tool.


---

## Why PainTalk?

Some people experience significant difficulty communicating pain verbally.

This can happen with:

- non-speaking or minimally speaking autistic people
- people with communication disabilities
- children
- older adults
- people recovering from neurological conditions
- people experiencing temporary difficulty speaking
- situations involving severe pain or distress

When a person cannot clearly explain where they hurt, caregivers, family members and professionals may have very limited information to work with.

PainTalk explores a simple alternative:

**What if a person could communicate the approximate location and intensity of pain visually?



## Project Motivation

The idea behind PainTalk comes from direct experience.

While working with autistic people, including people who were non-speaking or minimally verbal, I observed situations where someone was clearly experiencing distress or pain but had difficulty communicating where the pain was located.

In some situations, people could cry, hit themselves, bite themselves, or otherwise show that something was wrong without being able to explain the location or intensity of the pain.

I also experienced situations of severe pain myself where communicating the location of the pain was difficult.

These experiences led to a simple question:


> Could technology provide a simple visual way for someone to communicate pain without requiring speech?

PainTalk was created from that question.


## The Product

PainTalk uses a simple visual interaction flow.

```text
Body Map
   ↓
Body Region
   ↓
Detailed Location
   ↓
Pain Intensity
   ↓
Confirmation
   ↓
Communication
```

## Technology

PainTalk was built with:

- Flutter
- Dart
- Material 3
- Android
- SVG-based visual assets
- Local application state
- Offline-first design
- Text-to-speech support

The project intentionally avoids unnecessary backend infrastructure for the current beta.

## Engineering Approach

The project was developed through an AI-assisted engineering workflow.

AI was used to support:

- architecture exploration
- Flutter/Dart implementation
- UI iteration
- localization
- accessibility considerations
- documentation
- testing workflows
- development planning

Human direction remained responsible for product requirements, interaction decisions, validation and final acceptance.

The project therefore explores not only software development, but also AI-assisted software engineering and orchestration.


## Current Status

### Beta 1

PainTalk is currently in its first beta stage.

The current version focuses on:

- visual body-map interaction
- detailed body regions
- pain intensity communication
- accessibility
- multilingual support
- RTL support
- offline operation
- instructions/documentation
- Android physical-device testing

The application is being tested with real devices before broader distribution.
